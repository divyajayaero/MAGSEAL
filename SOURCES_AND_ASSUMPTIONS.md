# Evidence versus assumptions (audit sheet)

## Published demonstrations (independent systems)

- **NASA JSC (2023)**: Ionomer/elastomer material structures tested to stop leaks in spacecraft-relevant 6-mm openings, up to approximately 1 atm: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20230014767
- **MIT (2018)**: elastomer with embedded ferromagnetic microparticles changes shape under programmed fields: https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/0f6e4b3f-ade7-4057-8a6f-390405f815ec
- **ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces (2017)**: magnetic capsule guidance increased local capsule concentration by ~10 times in epoxy-fracture geometry, **not pressure-sealed holes**: https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.7b00459
- **Polymer (2020)**: magnetic field closes gaps in an Fe3O4-filled self-healing elastomer: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.polymer.2020.122819
- **Composite Structures (2021)**: magnetorheological resin used for pipeline/pressure vessel damage repair: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compstruct.2021.114566
- **ACS Applied Polymer Materials (2026)**: magnetic/UV adhesive repaired pipeline leaks up to 10 mm and 1.5 MPa, at 10–20 min total process time: https://doi.org/10.1021/acsapm.6c01921

## Assumptions deliberately *not* presented as measured MAGSEAL properties

- Whole-cabin lining divided into addressed 20-mm-radius cells and local access geometry.
- 6-mm ideal circular puncture, 2-mm hull depth, 12-mm-diameter inner collar.
- 0.25-mm reservoir, 50% recoverable, 80% delivery efficiency, 1.5-Pa*s Newtonian gel.
- 0.013116-A*m^2 effective moment on a 3-g NdFeB composite carrier; 2-mN static friction.
- Ideal air-core 200-turn, 1-A, 4-mm-radius coil 4 mm behind wall, no field interaction from neighbors.
- Localized puncture coordinates known perfectly once pressure sensor trips.
- Mechanical latch capacity 10 N; factor-of-3 static pressure-retention design target.
- Preliminary mobile head leaves 2% effective residual open area immediately after seating.
- Polymer cure 600 s; cured structure equivalent residual opening = 0.01%; neither is measured.
- Low-leak goal <=1 mg/s only a software screening threshold, **NOT equivalent to airtight or flight qualified**.

The model should be used to find *required* measurements, not as a substitute for obtaining them.
- **Nanomaterials (2020)**, comparison of CoFe2O4-core/BaTiO3-shell magnetic composites reports Ms 33.9 and Mr 5.98 A m^2/kg for one 60%-CoFe2O4 sample: https://www.mdpi.com/2079-4991/10/4/672 . Our MENP-only moment calculation assumes an illustrative 3-g carrier, 20% composite by mass, and 50% useful alignment. It is not a measured MAGSEAL actuation moment.

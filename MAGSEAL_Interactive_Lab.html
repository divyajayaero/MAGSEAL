Assumptions and what the animation depicts

The exterior sealing film is presumed to close according to an exponential recovery time constant; it is not a measured constitutive model. CoFe₂O₄–BaTiO₃ magnetic properties are represented by an estimated effective carrier moment of 0.001794 A·m². The coil is assumed accurately centered, with one 200-turn, 4-mm-radius coil positioned 4 mm from the repair plane. The carrier travels along an idealized guide. The estimated viscous outward extrusion uses a Hagen–Poiseuille screening law; polymer cure is an exponential fraction starting at carrier arrival. Gas leakage is calculated from an effective opening area proxy, not resolved pore geometry. A 3× static pressure-force screening factor is used for the exterior film/attachment capacity. The top-down and cross-section views depict these modeled states schematically and should not be interpreted as actual nanoscale structure or experimentally achieved material motion. All new settings are nonexperimental assumptions.
